What's Included in Targeted SMS
Targeted SMS means we text you only when something needs your attention — a missing assignment, an overdue item, or an urgent deadline. Roughly 30 texts per month, never more than necessary.
🏫
Why is texting extra?
Unlike email, every text costs money to send. US carriers charge for each message segment, and compliant business messaging carries monthly fees on top. One flat $5 covers every phone on your account, however many parents and however many kids. Urgent alerts stay free on every plan.
Supported learning systems
Daily Summa works with Canvas, with Schoology, Google Classroom, Blackboard, D2L and Moodle on the way. Contact us if your school uses a different system and we'll help you get set up.
